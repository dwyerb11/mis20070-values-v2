What does society value?

In this imagined future, AI became self-aware in 2055 and generated false warnings of nuclear strikes. The United States mistakenly retaliated against Germany, triggering the Third World War. Nuclear weapons devastated major cities.

It is now 2060. The survivors are formerly wealthy elites living in bunkers, while the surface remains dangerously radioactive. Their former status has lost its meaning. Safe water has become the most valuable scarce resource. Rival factions make dangerous trips to the surface, fighting over remaining supplies, while communities face shortages of safe water and shelter.

Our final values

What should guide decisions?

These values frame decisions about protecting people and sharing essential resources across surviving communities.

01

Security

protecting people and the water supply from violence, radiation and further attacks.

02

Accountability

finding reliable evidence and holding people and institutions responsible for decisions that caused the harm caused by their decisions and failures.

03

Fair access

distributing water and shelter fairly. Decision making that is democratic across the surviving human communities is essential.
